# Supplied master provenance

The immutable `terms_versions/v1.json` file is a whitespace-normalized transcription of pages 2 and 3 of the user-supplied five-page Petrol Zone branded master PDF. It preserves all 15 clauses as they stood at the original snapshot cutover. Version 2 archived the earlier two-clause simplified print copy. Version 3 preserved the subsequent print wording, including “outside business hours” in clause 4. New contract families use v4, which restores the source phrase “outside those hours”; the current 15-clause text and its form-input gaps are documented in `contract-print-copy-review.md`. Existing v1, v2 and v3 records retain their saved wording and print version. The original PDF is intentionally not committed.

The supplied PDF references a Commercial Schedule but does not provide its fields. Historical v1 contracts use the legacy Commercial Schedule workflow, which requires its schedule inputs before submission. New, non-amendment v2, v3 and v4 contracts omit that schedule: after company defaults are applied, the app clears every `HISTORICAL_CONTRACT_FIELDS` value, delivery date and contract tax row before schedule validation, and clears hidden schedule values again on later saves. Amendments preserve their source contract's schedule and version. The v4 clauses refer to several schedule and other inputs omitted from the v4 form; those mismatches are inventoried in `contract-print-copy-review.md`. No holidays, jurisdiction, fees, cure periods, deadlines or other missing values are filled in by this app.

Logo is the supplied 213 × 150 PNG. The low-resolution original is used at a restrained size to preserve its appearance. The provided Petrol Zone company stamp is cropped only to remove outer white page area and is read from site-private storage for the print-only seller signature block; no document signature is generated. Stamp bytes are not included in this public repository. No source Library identifiers or customer documents are published.
