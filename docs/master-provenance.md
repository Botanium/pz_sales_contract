# Supplied master provenance

The immutable `terms_versions/v1.json` file is a whitespace-normalized transcription of pages 2 and 3 of the user-supplied five-page Petrol Zone branded master PDF. It preserves all 15 clauses as they stood at the original snapshot cutover. Version 2 archived the earlier two-clause simplified print copy. New contract families now snapshot the user-supplied 15-clause v3 text, documented with its input gaps in `contract-print-copy-review.md`; existing v1 and v2 contracts retain their saved wording and print version. The original PDF is intentionally not committed.

The supplied PDF references a Commercial Schedule but does not provide its fields. Historical v1 contracts use the legacy Commercial Schedule workflow, which requires its schedule inputs before submission. New, non-amendment v2 and v3 contracts omit that schedule: after company defaults are applied, the app clears every `HISTORICAL_CONTRACT_FIELDS` value, delivery date and contract tax row before schedule validation, and clears hidden schedule values again on later saves. Amendments preserve their source contract's schedule and version. The v3 clauses themselves refer to several schedule and other inputs omitted from the v3 form; those mismatches are inventoried in `contract-print-copy-review.md`. No holidays, jurisdiction, fees, cure periods, deadlines or other missing values are filled in by this app.
