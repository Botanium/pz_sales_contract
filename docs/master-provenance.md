# Supplied master provenance

The immutable `terms_versions/v1.json` file is a whitespace-normalized transcription of pages 2 and 3 of the user-supplied five-page Petrol Zone branded master PDF. It preserves all 15 clauses as they stood at the original snapshot cutover. New contract families use the separately versioned, simplified v2 wording documented in `contract-print-copy-review.md`; historical contracts retain their stored wording and legacy layout. The original PDF is intentionally not committed.

The supplied PDF references a Commercial Schedule but does not provide its fields. Only historical v1 contracts use the legacy Commercial Schedule workflow, which captures those inputs explicitly and requires completion before submission. New v2 contract families omit the Commercial Schedule and clear any hidden legacy schedule values supplied through defaults, imports or REST requests; v2 validation does not derive deadlines from them. The app never supplies invented holidays, jurisdiction, fees, cure periods or collection deadlines. The supplied master states a 30% advance and 70% balance; approved v2 retains those percentages using discounted goods only.
