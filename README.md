# Petrol Zone Sales Contracts

A dedicated Frappe app for ERPNext v16. App users choose ERP Customers, Addresses, Contacts, Items, per-line Bitumen Grade masters, UOMs and Incoterms; app managers submit contracts and their linked native Sales Orders. The supplied branded master’s complete 15 clauses, agreed product specifications, Commercial Schedule, payment instructions and signature areas appear in the print/PDF.

## Payment DRAFT policy

For a customer without a valid finance history designation, the **first saved contract family per Customer, across companies** has a conspicuous payment DRAFT header until the full **30% advance** is evidenced. ERP `docstatus` is separate. Later contracts have no payment DRAFT header, even if the first remains unpaid. Consequently a second-contract-before-first-payment bypass is possible under the user’s first-contract-only policy; this app does not silently add a credit/release restriction.

The first family is reserved transactionally using a Customer row lock, a locking current registry read and a unique Customer index. A simultaneous save may require a retry under MariaDB snapshot isolation; the losing transaction rolls back rather than creating a second first family. Its customer/company cannot change; deletion is blocked. Native Customer rename preserves the unique Customer link reservation. Cancelled contracts retain the reservation and print CANCELLED. Cancellation also cancels the generated Sales Order transactionally; native permissions and active downstream payment/invoice/delivery links can block cancellation until finance unwinds them. Amendments inherit the first family but generate a new Sales Order and require allocations to that new order. An accidental first draft therefore remains in the audit trail: cancel/amend for the same customer, or create a later contract. This deliberate tradeoff prevents deletion/cancellation from resetting customer identity.

**Established customers:** finance can use **PZ Customer History** to register an identified prior signed contract/approval and a matching submitted native Sales Order. Accounts Manager (with native Sales Order/Account read access) or System Manager alone can submit/cancel these auditable records; Sales User and Sales Manager have no designation permission. Submission requires at least 30% of that historical order's total in qualifying native nominated-account receipts. A Customer master, old invoice balance or a manual paid checkbox is insufficient. No automatic production history scan is performed.

A submitted designation makes that Customer established across companies. Its prior receipt/reconciliation remains live evidence: refunds, cancellation, lost reconciliation or cancelled historical order restore the reserved app first family's DRAFT rule; the original reservation is retained. History records cannot be deleted, and the native receipt evidence at submission is recorded for audit. Orders generated by this app cannot be used as historical overrides. This migration route currently requires a native Sales Order and the same currency/payment constraints as current contracts; external-only documents or different historical advance percentages require a separately reviewed migration extension, rather than invented ERP evidence.

Finance migration steps: verify the prior contract and nominated accounts independently, open PZ Customer History, select Customer/Company/prior Sales Order, record the contract and review reference, and submit. Native receipt allocation and bank reconciliation (or agreed Cash receipt) must already be valid. Prefer completing these designations before sales starts new app contracts. Accounts Manager plus Sales User supplies the needed native order read access on a vanilla v16 site; any narrower site permission configuration must provide equivalent read access.

Payment evidence is computed live on the server and at print time. Receipts must be submitted, uncancelled native Payment Entries, positive Receive/Customer entries, correct company and party, received into the nominated Bank account or optional agreed Cash account on the contract, allocated to the submitted contract Sales Order or a submitted non-return invoice whose every line belongs to that order. Native advance reconciliation into such an invoice remains eligible. Each payment is counted once and capped by its receipt. Mixed-order invoices fail closed. Bank receipts require matching submitted deposit Bank Transactions, correct company/account/currency, full receipt reconciliation, live bank GL and clearance date. Manual clearance dates alone do not qualify. Cash receipts to genuine ERP Cash accounts use submitted finance accounting and live Cash GL as evidence of receipt. This is bookkeeping evidence, not independent physical verification.

Submitted Customer Pay refunds allocated to the order, its wholly attributable invoices or their credit notes reduce qualifying receipts once per payment, using absolute refund allocations and live Customer GL. Cancellation removes the refund deduction. A nonzero unallocated Customer payout for the same customer/company, an unsupported payout reference, a refund against a related mixed-order/foreign-currency invoice, or a related/unassigned Customer debit Journal Entry with live Cash/Bank outflow invalidates the evidence until finance resolves it. Fully allocated payouts against wholly unrelated orders/invoices do not affect this contract. Supported deductions do not require refund-bank reconciliation: an actual submitted refund must not leave the original advance qualified. These rules also apply to finance history; credit notes and Journal Entries never become qualifying incoming receipts.

**Foreign exchange limitation:** only receipts whose party and receiving accounts both use the contract currency and whose paid/received amounts match are eligible. Cross-currency receipts fail closed; there is no conversion based on a guessed exchange rate. Contracts themselves can use native ERP exchange rates. Journal Entries, payment requests, cancelled/return invoices, wallet credits and generic unallocated balances do not clear this marker. Pre-app receipt history establishes returning status only through the validated PZ Customer History finance workflow described above.

The marker is an anti-abuse cue, not a cryptographic signature or a guarantee that a downloaded PDF cannot be altered. Administrators and database operators remain trusted. Keep native payment and bank-reconciliation permissions restricted to finance. Managers must independently verify the printed bank instructions against the nominated receiving account before submission; ERP receipt evidence does not authenticate those instructions.

## Commercial inputs

The contract-entry form no longer asks for new deal-specific delivery arrangements, legal schedule or approval-record values. Company and generic Frappe defaults for these fields are cleared when the values were absent on a new contract, while existing schedule fields, legal clauses and populated historical data remain available for history and printing. A new contract PDF can therefore contain blank Commercial Schedule cells and “Not yet recorded” approval entries; it is not a complete expression of those omitted terms. No legal value is inferred or filled. For historical contracts with a complete schedule, overnight business hours remain unsupported, DST ambiguous boundaries use the first occurrence, nonexistent opening/closing times are rejected, and `24 business hours` means accumulated actual open hours. Historical notice timestamps require ISO 8601 offsets and reliable written delivery evidence; their deadlines use the saved Holiday List snapshot. Saving/submitting does not sign or send a contract.

The optional child `specification_reference` value is retained in stored history but hidden from the contract form and omitted from contract printing. Contract clauses are pinned at creation in a hidden `terms_snapshot`; amendments inherit the source clause snapshot. Pre-snapshot records print the immutable `terms_versions/v1.json` baseline rather than the mutable `terms.json`. A canonical SHA-256 check guards v1 at runtime and in the static test suite. The v1 file freezes the clause text present at this snapshot cutover; it cannot establish which text an older PDF used before snapshots existed. Proposed replacement print copy is in `docs/contract-print-copy-review.md` and remains inactive pending delivery, tax and legal review.

The supplied 30%/70% percentages are fixed because both the master’s legal clause and signature acknowledgement state them. Additional exclusive Actual or On Net Total taxes/charges and net-total discounts use native Sales Order calculations. Monetary print values use the native field precision; the server supplies advance precision to the payment-evidence dialog, including zero- and three-decimal currencies/settings. Inclusive taxes and compound tax rows are rejected explicitly. Each contract line selects an active Bitumen Grade master, independent of Item, and the link maps to Sales Order Item `custom_bitumen_grade`. The contract keeps a text snapshot for print/history: the master’s `grade_code` is used when that field exists, otherwise the canonical Link name is used. Previously saved free-text Grade values remain viewable and savable as history; an unchanged disabled Grade link remains available on a saved contract, while a new line cannot use disabled or missing masters. If a previously linked Grade master is missing, saving the unchanged contract clears that stale Link but retains its text snapshot. Agreed specification rows are optional at entry; Appendix A is omitted if none are recorded. No mixed-UOM quantity total is shown.

## Optional company defaults

A System Manager may maintain one **PZ Contract Defaults** record per seller Company. Defaults are copied server-side into blank supported seller, currency, price-list and receiving-account fields on a new, non-amendment contract only. Historical delivery/legal schedule settings remain in the settings schema but are not copied into new contracts. Deal-specific customer and product data, quantity, rate, Incoterm, named place, specification, discount and taxes are never defaulted. The Incoterm chooser always offers EXW, FOB and CIF; System Managers can add other existing ERP Incoterm records per Company. No Incoterm is auto-selected. Sales may change a copied seller value for an individual deal; each contract is validated by the existing link, currency and account checks.

Exchange rate, selling price list and receiving-account instructions are copied as a compatible bundle only when the contract currency matches the configured Company currency. A nonblank native or user-selected currency is never switched to match a profile. A dependent field that was blank in the submitted form may replace or clear an untouched generic Frappe default; explicit contract values are preserved. If configured currencies differ, configured bundle values are not copied and only blank-input fields filled by generic defaults are cleared. A legal-only profile with no currency-dependent settings leaves native defaults unchanged. When a contract explicitly supplies an incompatible list or account, the configured bundle is skipped and the existing strict link/currency validation rejects it; choose a matching currency, price list, exchange rate and accounts (or update the approved Company defaults) before saving.

If no defaults record exists, the original explicit-entry workflow remains available for fields still on the form. Changing Company on a new form clears the previous Company's seller address, signatory, currency and accounts before loading the newly selected Company's defaults. Each saved contract is self-contained: changing a defaults record later does not rewrite an existing draft, submitted contract, print or Sales Order. Defaults do not change the first-family DRAFT policy, payment evidence, roles or permissions.

Save is blocked while Company defaults are loading, applying or clearing. Wait for the update to finish, review the resulting values and save again. A blocked attempt never automatically saves later. Empty or failed optional-default lookups still permit manual entry once pending updates have finished.

## Installation (administrator action)

On a separately approved v16 bench, install the reviewed revision from `main` and verify its actual Git commit:

```sh
bench get-app https://github.com/Botanium/pz_sales_contract --branch main
bench --site YOUR_SITE install-app pz_sales_contract
bench --site YOUR_SITE migrate
bench build --app pz_sales_contract
```

Requires Frappe/ERPNext `>=16,<17`, Python 3.14 and the bench wkhtmltopdf PDF engine. Installation creates **PZ Sales Contract User** (create/read/write/print) and **PZ Sales Contract Manager** (those operations plus submit/cancel/amend). Assign the appropriate app role explicitly to approved users; installation and migration do not assign roles to any user. Administrator retains native full access. Generic Sales User, Sales Manager, Accounts Manager and System Manager roles do not grant access to PZ Sales Contract.

App roles grant no native Sales Order, master-data, payment, bank-reconciliation, finance-history or registry permissions. Reuse each approved user's existing native Sales Order create/submit/cancel and Customer/Company/Address/Contact/Item/Bitumen Grade/Account read access; the Sales Order Item `custom_bitumen_grade` field must already exist. Review any missing prerequisite separately. Finance history remains restricted to Accounts Manager/System Manager. Configure Company selling defaults, accounts, price lists, Grade and Incoterm masters in ERPNext. Enable draft print in native Print Settings if preliminary contracts must be printed.

All supported native contract print formats are routed through the mandatory app template with a freshly loaded persisted document. Native beta print builders, direct WeasyPrint downloads and the optional Chrome PDF renderer are explicitly rejected at configuration/request boundaries; scalar and dictionary batch requests are covered. Unsaved print payloads, client-supplied paid flags and alternate Standard formats cannot remove the marker from app-generated output. Other DocTypes retain Frappe’s ordinary print behavior.
